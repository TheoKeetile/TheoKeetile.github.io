Featured work

RoadAid, a vehicle recovery and roadside assistance platform built for Addis Ababa and deployed live.

Built over a four-month MSc dissertation with a team of four, commissioned by AB Analytics Ltd. The platform replaces manual dispatch with an automated engine that combines nearest-neighbour matching for single requests and the Hungarian algorithm for requests arriving together, with priority weighting for urgent jobs and automatic cascade when an operator declines.

My own share was the client-facing layer: the cancellation policy and fee model, fare and earnings screens, job history and summary views, bilingual English and Amharic localisation, and Ethiopian phone validation.
